# Presentation speaking guide

The seven English slides have short Arabic notes in Presenter View. [One-page Arabic notes PDF](../deliverables/Tayseer_Presenter_Notes_AR.pdf).

| Slide | Question and answer | Suggested time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | What approval? SAR 40M across eight laggards, with 35M for the four widest gaps and 5M for the others. Ask within the first 30 seconds. | 35 sec |
| 2 | National target met? Yes, 66.21%, 1.21 points above target, up about 12 points since January 2022. | 45 sec |
| 3 | Regional problem? Eight of 13 below target. Dark bars mean Below Target and the orange line means 65%. Al-Baha is included. | 50 sec |
| 4 | Why these four? They have the widest gaps and 86.96%, rounded to 87%, of the sum of positive gaps. | 50 sec |
| 5 | What options? Equal 5M per laggard, concentrated 10M in each of four, or recommended gap-based 35M/5M coverage. | 45 sec |
| 6 | How calculated? Allocate by gaps and round to half a million with largest remainders, totaling 40M. A numeric causal improvement is not established. | 60 sec |
| 7 | Next steps? Approve and appoint a lead, validate costs/plans within 30 days before spending, and review adoption, spending and service quality after six months. Repeat the ask. | 40 sec |

Suggested total: 5 minutes 25 seconds, leaving transitions within seven minutes. Actual timing requires rehearsal.

If asked about the measure: sum row adoption × users, then divide by total user weights. Data are synthetic and users may overlap across records.
